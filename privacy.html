Privacy Policy

EMasbaha

Last updated: November 21, 2026

EMasbaha is designed to be private. It has no accounts and asks for no personal information. The only data that leaves your device is a small amount of anonymous information we share with Meta to measure our ads, described below.

Data shared with Meta to measure our ads

We advertise EMasbaha on Facebook and Instagram. To know whether those ads work, the app uses Meta’s SDK to send Meta only:

  • that the app was installed, opened and closed, and how long it was open (Meta uses this to count installs), and
  • that you bought the one-time unlock, with its price and currency.

With these events Meta receives technical information about your device, such as the device model, operating system and app version, and your IP address. On iPhone this includes a device identifier that is specific to EMasbaha, but never Apple’s advertising identifier: the app does not ask to track you. On Android, Meta also receives your device’s advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your phone’s settings.

This data is not linked to your name or any account, and it is used only to measure and improve our ads. Your dhikr, counts and settings are never sent. Meta handles the data under its own Privacy Policy.

We do not collect any other data, and we do not sell your data.

Data stored on your device

Your counts, today’s total and your settings (language, dhikr, target, sound, haptics, appearance, hand, masbaha position and bead style) are saved only on your device so the app remembers them. They may be included in your device’s own iCloud or Google backup, under your control. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

The free trial and the one-time unlock are handled entirely by Apple’s App Store (or Google Play). We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the store whether you have unlocked it.

Children

EMasbaha does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information. The ad-measurement events above are sent for every user and contain no personal details.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
