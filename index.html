A digital masbaha for iPhone that feels like real prayer beads. Swipe a bead with your thumb to count your dhikr.

Common questions

How do I count? Swipe a bead up or down on the strand. Each bead counts once.

How do I move the beads under my thumb? Open Settings → Masbaha position and drag the slider.

How does pricing work? Every feature is free for 3 days. After that, a single one-time payment of $1.99 unlocks EMasbaha for life. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically.

I bought it on another iPhone. Tap “Restore purchase” on the trial screen or the unlock screen.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
